<h2 style="color: #ffffff; margin-bottom: 1.5rem; font-size: 1.5rem;">About Janne Ojajärvi</h2>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
I am a Finnish musician and educator dedicated to the ancient sound of the Nordic <strong>overtone flute</strong>. My passion lies in bridging the gap between centuries-old Nordic traditions and modern musical expression.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
With a Master's degree in Folk Music and a background in sound design, I perform with a wide range of instruments, including harmonicas, jaw harps (mouth harps), and throat singing.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
With years of experience in performing and teaching the <strong>willow flute</strong> (pitkähuilu), I created this platform to be a global home for enthusiasts—especially for the growing community of world music lovers in the <strong>United States</strong> and beyond.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
Whether you are a beginner looking for your first tutorial or a professional searching for authentic sheet music, I am here to guide you through the unique harmonic world of this finger-holeless instrument.
</p>

<ul style="line-height: 1.8; margin-bottom: 1.5rem; padding-left: 1.2rem;">
  <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong>Expertise:</strong> Nordic folk music, overtone series, willow flute & harmonica pedagogy, improvisation.</li>
  <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem;"><strong>Mission:</strong> To provide high-quality, accessible resources for the global overtone flute community.</li>
</ul>
