---
title: "Posture and playing position"
---

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
The foundational posture for playing a side-blown overtone flute involves supporting the instrument with one hand while using the opposite hand to open and close the distal end. For instance, although right-handed, the author typically supports the instrument with the right hand and controls the end with the left. 
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
Maintaining a healthy, upright posture is essential. Keep your shoulders relaxed and aligned backward, and engage your core abdominal muscles to support an upright torso. Ensure your arms are held slightly away from your body so that your lungs can expand and your breath flows freely. Rather than slouching or pushing your head forward to reach the instrument, bring the flute smoothly up to meet your mouth.
</p>
