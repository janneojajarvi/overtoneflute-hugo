---
title: "Breathing"
---

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
Proper deep breathing originates in the diaphragm and expands upward through the upper lobes of the lungs. When exhaling, the breath should be steadily supported starting from the lower respiratory area. Good breathing habits can be cultivated by practicing sustained tones with focused awareness, while natural inhalation techniques develop over time through regular playing exercises and tunes. 
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">
As a starting point, you can test how long you can sustain a melody before needing a fresh breath. Natural phrasing points often serve as ideal places to breathe, though players always retain the freedom to breathe wherever it suits their musical expression.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 1.05rem; line-height: 1.6; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
Optimal airflow control is achieved when your lungs are never completely full or completely empty. Strive to maintain a comfortable buffer where your lungs remain at least one-fifth full and one-fifth empty at all times.
</p>
